Spidey Sense

A sixth sense for what you missed.

What
Smart glasses that tap your wrist about things you haven't looked at.
How
The glasses track where your eyes are, Claude spots what matters, and a small Dot buzzes only if you missed it.
Why
Our eyes are often on a phone or lost in thought. One tap at the right moment means you see the cable, the box or the fist in time.

Under the hood

For the curious

What it's made of
  • Pupil Labs Neon eye-tracking glasses, streaming through their Companion phone's hotspot to a Mac.
  • Claude Haiku checks every frame against your triggers; your real gaze decides whether you noticed.
  • DataFeel Dot on the wrist over Bluetooth: vibration and a colour per trigger.
  • iPhone app with Spidey's voice (ElevenLabs) and talk-to-add triggers. Optional Oura heart rate.
How fast and how accurate
Glasses video to the Mac~90 ms
Something in view to a buzz, in real takes1–2 s
Dot buzz over Bluetooth117 ms
Obstacles caught in 56 hand-labelled frames96%
Fist frames caught after rewording the trigger14 / 14
What doesn't work yet
  • The glasses' microphone gives no audio, so hearing is built but off.
  • Oura readings are only as fresh as the ring's last sync.
  • The Mac does the thinking, so it rides along in a backpack.
