The problem

We only notice
what we look at.

The dangerous thing is usually the one outside your attention.

The blind spot

A bike closing in on your left while you watch the road ahead.

Behind you

Someone walking up from behind, where no eye can see.

Near the water

A child drifting toward the pool edge while you look away.

Attention is a spotlight: we only really notice what we look at. The things that hurt us are usually outside that spotlight, like a bike in the blind spot, someone coming up behind, or a kid edging toward the water.
