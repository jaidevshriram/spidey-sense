SPIDEY SENSEPART 2 · HOW IT WORKS
The Dot has three channels: vibration, light and heat. We built a small vocabulary: urgent is a sharp triple click and strong buzz in red, pulse is a heartbeat in pink, ambient is a soft blue hum. Intensity works by swapping to weaker waveforms, and warmth is hard-capped at 40 degrees with automatic shut-offs.
