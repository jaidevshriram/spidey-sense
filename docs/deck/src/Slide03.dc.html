SPIDEY SENSEPART 1 · PITCH
Three pieces. The glasses see what you see and where your eyes are. Claude looks at each frame and asks: is there something here you haven't noticed? If so, a small dot on your body buzzes, glows, or warms up, so you get the message without looking at a screen.
